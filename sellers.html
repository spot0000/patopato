Questions sellers ask

Where can I sell?

At markets and fairs, in your shop, and online: Etsy, Amazon, TikTok Shop and your own website.

Is there a limit on how many I can sell?

No limit. Print the toys yourself and sell as many as you like.

Can I sell or share the 3D files?

No. The license covers physical prints only. Please don't sell, share or re-upload the files.

What happens if I cancel?

The license is valid while your subscription is active. After you cancel, please stop selling new prints.

Which toys are included?

80+ Patopato toys, including the paid toys for Patreon members. New toys are added every month.

How do I get my certificate?

After you subscribe, send your name and shop name. Your certificate arrives by email.

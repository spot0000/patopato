I make toys that make you go "wow" or "haha". A toilet that launches poop. Pop-Eyed characters: press them and the eyes pop out. Air-pressure rockets that hit the ceiling. Tanks that kids play with for hours.

My boys like every one of them. After the first tank, there was ammo all over our floor. So the next tank got an ammo box 😆

Even the springs are 3D printed.

If you like one, print an extra one for a friend. I once gave 20 or 30 little crocodiles to my son's class.
